# NetworkTests

**Free network diagnostics — find out what's actually wrong with your connection, and prove it.**

- 🌐 **[networktests.com](https://networktests.com)** — instant browser tests: packet loss, latency, jitter, speed, MTR & reverse path, DNS leak, TLS interception check. Nothing to install.
- 🖥️ **[NetworkTests Monitor](https://github.com/networktests/networktests)** — a desktop app for **macOS, Windows and Linux** that watches your connection every second and tells you in plain English where problems start: your Wi-Fi, your router, your ISP, your computer — or the app you're using. Free for personal use, no account, all data stays on your machine.
